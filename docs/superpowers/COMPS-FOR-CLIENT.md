# Three home-page design directions — for review

We built three alternative layouts for the home page so you can compare structure, not
words. All three use the exact same copy as the live site — every sentence is locked to
the wording you already approved, because of the FDA-exposure language on the site. So
this is a comparison of layout and pacing only, never a copy test.

Each link opens the comp already set to a suggested colour palette, but the palette picker
at `/themes` can re-skin any comp — palette and layout are independent choices.

## The links

- **Editorial** — `/designs/editorial?theme=` (default Sage & Clay palette)
- **Clinical** — `/designs/clinical?theme=ocean`
- **Photographic** — `/designs/photographic?theme=sunrise`

## Three things to know before you choose

**1. Photographic is undersold by the assets we have today.**
The site currently has one lifestyle photograph, and it's only 721px wide — visibly soft
once it's stretched to fill a hero banner. Choosing this direction commits you to
commissioning new photography; the comp as it stands is a preview of the *idea*, not the
final quality. Separately, the dark overlay behind the white headline on that photo is
set unusually heavy — 80% opacity — because that's what it takes to keep the text
readable against the brightest part of the photo. That heaviness is a deliberate
accessibility choice, not a mistake we plan to lighten.

**2. Editorial repeats a sentence on purpose.**
The pulled-out quote in that layout — "PEMF is a holistic approach to promote a state of
total wellness." — also appears in the paragraph just above it. That repetition is an
intentional editorial device (pull quotes are meant to echo the text around them), not an
oversight.

**3. Clinical shows "Certified PEMF Expert Sharon" twice.**
Once just under the hero, as a credential, and again at the very bottom of the page, as a
signature. That's deliberate — the two mentions do different jobs at opposite ends of a
long page — not a duplication we missed.
